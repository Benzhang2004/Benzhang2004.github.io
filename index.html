Jialin Zhang

Computer Science and Applied Mathematics, UC Berkeley

I am a senior at UC Berkeley studying Computer Science and Applied Mathematics. I work with Prof. Matei Zaharia and Prof. Dawn Song.

I'm broadly interested in self-improving LLM agents. I study how to build realistic evaluations for complex AI systems, how agents can evaluate and improve themselves, and how such systems scale efficiently.
